# Privacy Policy - Biblia Focus

**Last updated: June 2026**

## Overview

Biblia Focus ("we", "our", "extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This extension blocks entertainment websites and displays Bible verses. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

## Data Collection

**We do NOT collect any personal data.** Specifically:

- We do NOT collect browsing history
- We do NOT collect personal information
- We do NOT collect location data
- We do NOT communicate with any external servers
- We do NOT use cookies or tracking technologies
- We do NOT share any data with third parties

## Local Storage

The extension uses Chrome's local storage API exclusively to store:

- **Allowed sites with timers**: When you temporarily allow a blocked site for 30 minutes, the expiration time is stored locally in your browser. This data never leaves your device.

## Bible Data

All Bible verses (NVI - Nueva Versión Internacional) are stored locally within the extension. No internet connection is required to display verses or read chapters.

## Permissions

The extension requests the following permissions and uses them solely as described:

- **webNavigation**: To detect when you navigate to a blocked website and redirect to the verse page
- **tabs**: To update the tab URL when redirecting blocked sites
- **storage**: To store the temporary allow-list for the 30-minute access feature
- **alarms**: To automatically revoke temporary site access after 30 minutes
- **host_permissions (<all_urls>)**: To check all URLs against the blocklist; no data is sent externally

## Third Parties

This extension does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics, or advertising networks.

## Children's Privacy

This extension does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date.
